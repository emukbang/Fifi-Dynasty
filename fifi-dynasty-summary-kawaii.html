How to read this page

A quick primer before you dive in — it'll make everything below make a lot more sense.

The five traits

The Big Five breaks personality into five ingredients everyone has in different amounts: Extraversion, Agreeableness, Conscientiousness, Neuroticism, and Openness. Each one splits into six more specific facets.

What the numbers mean

Scores are percentiles from 0–99. A score of 80 on a trait means that person is higher on it than about 80% of people — it's relative, not a grade. Nothing here is good or bad, just different.

The shapes and colors

Each radar chart is a personality "shape" — the bigger the shape reaches toward an edge, the higher that score. The heatmap tables use color instead of shape: white is low, hot pink is high.

Comparing people

Click one or more names in any name list to spotlight that whole group everywhere on the page — on every chart and every table at once. Click a name again to release just that one, or hover a name for a quick peek.
